Privacy Policy

Noll Household Alerts is a private notification program run by Danny Noll for his immediate household.

What we collect: the mobile phone number you use to text START, and the messages you send to the program.

How we use it: only to send the notifications you signed up for and to respond to HELP and STOP requests.

Sharing: We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Opting out: reply STOP at any time. Your number will receive no further messages.
