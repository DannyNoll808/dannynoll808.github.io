Noll Household Alerts

Noll Household Alerts sends account notifications by text message to Danny Noll and members of his immediate household who choose to sign up.

To sign up, text START to +1 (859) 694-9201.

What you will receive

  • A daily schedule summary
  • Bill due-date reminders
  • Alerts about unusual account activity
  • Household appointment reminders

Message frequency varies, typically 1 to 5 messages per day.

Message and data rates may apply.

Help and opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. You can also email danny.a.noll@gmail.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
